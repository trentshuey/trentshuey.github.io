Book a Marimba Recital

Solo marimba for schools, universities, churches, and concert series, from music written for the instrument's leading players to new works he premiered.

Repertoire

Dr. Shuey's doctoral research at the University of Illinois is a performance guide to five marimba works commissioned by William Moersch, and his recitals draw on that music alongside standards of the solo literature.
