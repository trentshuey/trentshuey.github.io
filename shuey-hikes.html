Shuey Hikes

Camping, backpacking, and long road trips through the national parks, filmed on a 360 camera and shared on YouTube.

Watch on YouTube Instagram

Out on the trail

Dr. Shuey's love of the outdoors comes from his dad, Paul, a lifelong backpacker and outdoorsman. Shuey Hikes is where the trips end up: canyon wades, alpine passes, sunrise campsites, and the long drives in between.

It is the same curiosity he brings to the practice room, pointed at a trail map instead of a score.

The 21-day road trip

Nine states and five national parks in three weeks, solo, now becoming a twelve-part series.

  1. Manzano MountainsNew Mexico
  2. SedonaArizona, with family
  3. Grand CanyonArizona
  4. ZionUtah: three nights, the Narrows in floodwater, and Scout's Lookout
  5. Bryce CanyonUtah: hoodoo country
  6. Snake RiverBurley, Idaho: sunrise over the water
  7. HermistonOregon: home, family, and old photos
  8. EugeneOregon: up Spencer Butte
  9. Ainsworth State ParkOregon: the Columbia River Gorge
  10. Mount HoodOregon
  11. Grand TetonWyoming: Colter Bay
  12. Colorado SpringsColorado: Garden of the Gods

West Maroon Pass

A June 2026 backpack with friends from Crested Butte to Aspen over West Maroon Pass, by way of the Copper Creek Trail and East Maroon Pass, with a swim in Copper Lake along the way.

The gear got its shakedown first at San Angelo State Park.

In the pack

  • Insta360 X4 AirThe camera behind every episode
  • Mountainsmith Bear Creek 2Tent
  • Sawyer SqueezeWater filter
  • MSR DynaLock Ascent CarbonTrekking poles
  • Garmin inReach Mini 2Satellite messenger

How the videos are made

Shot in 360 on an Insta360 X4 Air, reframed in Insta360 Studio, and cut in Adobe Premiere Pro. The Zion episode is scored with Dr. Shuey's own original music.
