A performance guide to five marimba works

Doctoral dissertation, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, 2020

Dr. Shuey's dissertation, A Performance Guide to the Marimba Music of Jared Beynon, Harold Farberman, James Lauth, Akmal Parwez, and Steven Rosenhaus, examines five works commissioned by his teacher and advisor William Moersch. It was accompanied by a recital that included three world premieres.

  • Devil DogJared Beynon
  • “A” TrainHarold Farberman
  • Sonata for MarimbaJames Lauth
  • Banyan BoughsAkmal Parwez
  • Mission MusicSteven Rosenhaus
