Conversations

A music and visual art collaboration with artist Kat Truth Bunker.

{{keys}}

He plays what he sees. She draws what she hears.

In Conversations, two artists take turns answering each other. Dr. Shuey improvises on percussion in response to Kat Truth Bunker's artwork, and she draws in response to what she hears. Each piece is shaped by listening rather than by a score.

The collaboration has produced seven works, all recorded below.
