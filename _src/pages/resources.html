Join the Percussive Arts Society

Join the Percussive Arts Society at www.pas.org: $50 a year for members 26 and under, $75 standard.

PASIC, the Percussive Arts Society International Convention, runs each November in Indianapolis. PASIC 2026 is November 11–14. You are strongly encouraged to attend.

Buying music and gear

Buy your music and books. Photocopies are illegal and not acceptable; failing to obtain assigned music reduces your grade. The required books, sticks, mallets, and accessories are listed in the handbook.
