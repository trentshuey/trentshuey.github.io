Big band and small group, every semester

ASU's jazz program gives players both big band and small group experience, across swing, bebop, Latin jazz, funk, and free improvisation. The Big Band is a scholarship ensemble chosen by audition. Jazz combos run every semester, because improvising is learned in small groups as much as in a big band.

In fall 2023, ASU ran two big bands at once for the first time, and the program performs on campus and around town, from the Blue Buffalo Art Gallery to Brooks & Bates.

Jam sessions at the House of FiFi DuBois

Since 2022, ASU's jazz combos have hosted jam sessions at the House of FiFi DuBois on Chadbourne Street in downtown San Angelo. On a Saturday afternoon the combos play from 4:00 to 5:00, then the stage opens to musicians from the community until 6:00.

Every level is welcome, and Dr. Shuey often joins the combos on vibraphone.
