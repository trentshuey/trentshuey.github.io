Studio Class

Every meeting follows the same shape, so you always know what to have ready when you walk in: a studio recital, where scheduled performers play, followed by structured peer feedback; then announcements and room reset.

Bring sticks and mallets, a pencil, and your music to every session. Performances are graded on preparation and growth, not perfection.

Recital slots rotate, but every student performs at least once in each unit: snare, mallets, timpani, and drum set or multiple percussion.

Expectations

Perform

Everyone performs in Studio Class and Percussion Ensemble. Percussion Ensemble is an essential lab experience of applied study; majors do not audition.

Practice

B.M. and B.A. students practice two hours a day. Non-majors, one hour.

Come prepared

Bring sticks, mallets, music, a pencil, and your lesson log to every lesson. Keep a lesson notebook: assignments, technical and musical comments, goals, studio class materials, practice logs.

Show up

You have two free absences across lessons, studio class, and ensemble combined. Give notice by the night before if you cannot attend a lesson. Attend every equipment move for any ensemble you are in.

Practice rooms

Room 288 is the main percussion room: the 5.0-octave Yamaha YM-5100 marimba, Musser vibraphone, four Ludwig timpani, the snare collection, drum sets, and storage.

Three further rooms hold a 4.3-octave Musser kelon marimba, a 4.0-octave Musser rosewood, and a xylophone. Room 134 is also available, with a full complement.

Sign up on the posted schedule for up to two hours a day. A reserved slot may be taken over if you are more than ten minutes late. Lights off, room empty.

Skills Assessment Test

You must pass the Skills Assessment Test before performing the Junior Recital (MUAP 3199) or Senior Recital (MUAP 4199). It is offered once per semester, during jury week.

You prepare everything; the panel selects. Every key, all forty rudiments, and all three instruments must be ready.

Senior recital

  • Pass the Skills Assessment Test first.
  • A recital hearing 30 days before the recital, for a three-member faculty panel. Passing the hearing exempts you from the jury.
  • A performance on a Departmental Student Recital.
  • A poster and a printed program with program notes.
