Rudiments

All 40 Percussive Arts Society International Drum Rudiments. Press play to hear any rudiment at your tempo and follow the sticking as it plays.

{{keys}}

All 40 are on the Skills Assessment Test. Each must be played from memory, eight times in succession without error, and the panel draws four. Draw four picks four at random, just like the test.

Start slowly enough for complete accuracy, then raise the tempo in steps only while accuracy holds. Right-hand strokes sound slightly to the right and left-hand strokes slightly to the left, so you can hear the sticking with headphones.
