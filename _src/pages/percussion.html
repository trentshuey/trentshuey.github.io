The whole instrument family

The studio covers the whole instrument family: snare drum, all keyboard percussion, timpani, multiple percussion, drum set, orchestral accessories, and Latin and world percussion. Lessons emphasize orchestral performance, period-correct approaches to each piece, all keyboard percussion, multiple percussion, and jazz. Students prepare for careers in performance and music education, and the studio has a 100% placement rate for graduates in their chosen fields.

Non-majors are welcome in lessons and ensembles. Whatever you want from percussion, there is a place for you here.

Private lessons

Every percussion student at Angelo State studies one on one with Dr. Shuey, every week, in lessons built around where you are and where you want to go.

Lessons are taught from traditional notation, the way you will read music in an orchestra, a band, a jazz band, or a pit, with the period-correct approach to each piece and a plan for how to practice it.

  • Snare drum and all 40 PAS rudiments
  • Timpani, from tuning to the orchestral repertoire
  • Marimba and all keyboard percussion, two and four mallets
  • Multiple percussion and orchestral accessories
  • Drum set and jazz
  • Latin and world percussion

Learn from a working player: principal timpani with the Amarillo Symphony, section percussion with the West Texas Symphony, timpani soloist in Philip Glass's Concerto Fantasy, and percussionist for Tito Puente Jr. Graduates of the studio have a 100% placement rate in their chosen fields.

Where you'll play

  • Percussion Ensemble {{img:logo-percussion-ensemble}}

    Two or more concerts a year plus school outreach. Recent programs include Glimmer (fall 2025) in ASU's newly renovated auditorium and House Music (spring 2026), built around John Cage's Living Room Music and Thierry De Mey's Table Music.

  • Ram Band Drumline {{img:logo-ram-band}}{{img:logo-drumline}}

    Every battery arrangement is written by Dr. Shuey, who has arranged percussion for more than 40 halftime shows. The line has grown from 8 members to 21 in the 2026 season.

  • Jazz Ensembles {{img:logo-jazz-ensemble}}{{img:logo-jazz-combos}}

    Drummers and percussionists are needed every semester in the ASU Big Band and jazz combos.

  • Ram Horns {{img:logo-ram-horns-stacked}}

    Dr. Shuey created the Ram Horns name and identity in 2016 and has directed the group since, with drummers and percussionists in the band.

  • Wind Ensemble and Symphony Orchestra

    Percussionists also perform with ASU's major ensembles.

  • ASU Day of Percussion

    An annual event Dr. Shuey founded in 2017 that brings percussionists to campus.
