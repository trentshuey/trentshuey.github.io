ÓperaMaya

An international music festival on Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula.

{{keys}}

Dr. Shuey joined the ÓperaMaya International Music Festival in 2018 and served on its percussion faculty through 2023, teaching and coaching percussion students and performing with the festival orchestra alongside musicians from around the world.

The festival was cancelled in 2020 because of the COVID-19 pandemic, making five seasons in all. He received the festival's percussion scholarship in 2021.
