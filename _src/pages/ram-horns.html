Pep band for the Rams and Rambelles

The Ram Horns are a high-energy spirit ensemble that plays home basketball games for the Rams and Rambelles, plus select university and community engagements. Dr. Shuey created the Ram Horns name and identity in 2016 and has directed the group since.

You do not have to be a music major to play. Auditions are open to every ASU student on a brass instrument, saxophone, or drum set.
