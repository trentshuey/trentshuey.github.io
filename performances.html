Projects

  • ÓperaMaya

    Five seasons on the percussion faculty of an international music festival on Mexico's Yucatán Peninsula.

  • Conversations

    Seven works made in dialogue with visual artist Kat Truth Bunker. He plays what he sees; she draws what she hears.

  • Fort Concho

    Official Drummer of the historic fort in San Angelo, playing an original 1867 drum.

Jazz and beyond

Jazz vibraphone with the ASU combos at the House of FiFi DuBois, steel drums with the I-Pan steel band, and recordings with the Dakota Brown Band and the PS Jazz Quartet.
