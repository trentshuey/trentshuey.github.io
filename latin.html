How the Afro-Cuban percussion section locks together around the clave. Press play, then turn parts on and off to hear what each one adds.

Bongos
FtFtFtOtFtFtFtOt

Steady eighth notes: fingers (F) and thumb (t) on the small drum, an open tone (O) on the large drum on 4.

Güiro

A long scrape (L) on 1 and 3, with two short strokes (s) between.

Bass

Each part is shown in its basic form; players vary every one of them. The grid is two bars of eighth notes, with the clave's three side and two side marked above. Sounds are synthesized in your browser, so treat them as a guide and listen to the records for the real thing.

Where this comes from

Dr. Shuey has played bongos and mambo bell for Tito Puente Jr., and the ASU jazz program has given a full semester to Afro-Cuban jazz, mambo, and salsa built on the clave. Percussion students learn these parts in lessons and play them in the jazz combos and the Big Band.
